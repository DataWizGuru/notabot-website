# Applied design direction
The reviewed UX architecture uses independent static pages, direct and optional guided routes, contextual enquiry drafts and transparent estimates. Current styling uses near-black surfaces, a restrained blue glow, warm serif emphasis, the supplied NotABot logo, readable white body content and responsive grids. Functional behaviour lives independently in ux-core.js and ux.js. Do not introduce unverified testimonials, metrics, course availability or fixed consultancy prices. A future style guide can replace presentation without changing the journeys.


Brand correction: gold #D4AF37; dark background/gradient #0B1220. Header/footer use the supplied official logo, and Home uses the supplied portrait mascot without cropping.
