THE CASE OF THE MISSING SALESPart 1 of 4
THE ALERT · WHAT HAPPENED?

The dashboard flashes red. The room blames marketing.

Friday revenue is down 12%. The quickest idea is to cut advertising—but the number only tells us what changed.

Your move: investigate before you react.
THE INVESTIGATION · WHY?

Three clues refuse to fit the first story.

  • Website traffic is steady.
  • Orders placed are almost unchanged.
  • The drop is concentrated in one fulfilment region.
Better question: did demand fall—or did completed revenue move?
THE REVEAL · WHAT MAY HAPPEN NEXT?

The missing sales are sitting in a depot—not in the market.

A dispatch delay held completed orders past Friday’s cut-off. Demand is healthy; fulfilment is late.

Plot twist: cutting marketing would attack the wrong problem and weaken next week’s demand.
THE DECISION · WHAT SHOULD WE DO?

Clear the backlog. Protect demand. Watch recovery.

The team fixes dispatch capacity, tracks delayed orders and keeps marketing active. That is Analytics 101: turning evidence into understanding and action.
