Notabot exists because data should feel clear, useful, and human.

Businesses do not need more dashboards for the sake of dashboards. They need decision experiences that make people feel confident about what they are seeing and what they should do next.

Our story

Notabot Studio was created at the intersection of analytics, storytelling, and human-centred design. The name is a reminder that intelligent systems can power the work, but the creativity, empathy, judgement, and clarity still come from people.


We help teams turn reporting chaos into decision-ready Power BI and Microsoft Fabric experiences through consulting, dashboard UX/UI, training, and analytics advisory.


Every engagement is guided by a simple idea: data should make decisions easier, not make people feel less capable.

“We make data feel natural, stories feel powerful, and people feel confident.”
