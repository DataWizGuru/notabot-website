Our approach

A clear way
to move forward.

Start with the problem and the people. These stages explain how we think about the work; they are not compulsory steps you must complete on this website.

From understanding to ownership

  1. Discover

    Start with the people, the situation and the decisions that matter.

    What this involves

    Bring your questions, desired outcome and the context in which people work.

  2. Assess

    Understand the data, current capability and practical constraints.

    What this involves

    We discuss what is available, where the gaps are and what needs more exploration.

  3. Design

    Agree a useful approach around the outcome.

    What this involves

    The solution or learning scope should have a clear audience and purpose.

  4. Build

    Put the agreed work into practice.

    What this involves

    Delivery depends on the scope: practical learning, a working solution, or both.

  5. Enable

    Support understanding and ownership.

    What this involves

    Discuss how people will use what has been learned or built, and what support is included.

What should I bring?

Your goal, what feels difficult, who is involved and any constraints on time, tools or data. You do not need a technical specification or a chosen service to begin.
